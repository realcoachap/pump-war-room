The 3-minute workflow

  1. Check the feed.

    Confirm the top status says LIVE and inspect “Last real mint.” STALE or CONNECTING means the screen may not represent current activity.

  2. Scan Top 100 Radar.

    Start with the default evidence/recency lens. Search by name, ticker, or exact mint; use freshness and evidence filters to narrow the tape.

  3. Open a coin.

    Select a row to inspect its exact mint, supporting momentum observations, named risk factors, identity resolution, outcomes, and retained timeline.

  4. Build a watchlist.

    Star coins worth revisiting. Your list and saved filter lenses stay in this browser only. Select two to four watched coins for a side-by-side evidence comparison.

  5. Ask Caesar.

    Use a quick brief or ask a focused question. Caesar summarizes only the War Room snapshot available at that moment and cites the observations behind the answer.

Best habit: treat —, unavailable, stale, and insufficient evidence as “we do not know”—never as zero, safe, or bad.
NEW IN v0.10

Canonical identity graph

Memecoins can reuse names, tickers, images, and narratives across different contracts. The identity graph keeps the exact Solana mint as the source of truth while allowing reviewed relationships between mints.

Exact mint first

Every mint resolves independently. Two tokens with the same branding remain separate unless a human-reviewed relationship says otherwise.

Entity + variants

A reviewed entity may connect an official launch, migration, or relaunch and may name a primary mint when the evidence is unambiguous.

Proposals are not facts

Automated name, ticker, or narrative collisions are visibly labeled proposals. They cannot canonize an identity, change rank, or become a recommendation.

Bounded by design

The pending proposal backlog is hard-capped at 500. Stale pending suggestions are pruned while reviewed decisions remain retained.

Primary mint means identity resolution only. It does not mean authentic, safe, high quality, or worth buying.

Shows whether real mint ingestion and browser updates are current.

Top 100 Radar
Ranks only observed mints using supported evidence, falling back to recency when evidence is thin.
Identity registry
Separates exact contracts from reviewed project, migration, relaunch, collision, or copycat relationships.
Workbench
Stores browser-local watchlists and filters, compares exact mints, and shows closed-period briefs.

Displays named factors and unknowns; no composite safety probability is calculated.
Early actors
Shows bounded, privacy-preserving activity summaries when the evidence gate is met; wallets are not exposed.
Outcomes
Measures completed provider candles after observation. Missing or immature windows stay unavailable.
FAQ

Common questions

Is this financial advice or a trading bot?

No. It is read-only observational research. There is no wallet connection, order execution, or recommendation engine.

Does Top 100 mean the whole Pump.fun market?

No. It means the highest-ranked mints inside this deployment’s observed feed and evidence contract.

Why do I see dashes or “unavailable”?

The required evidence is missing, immature, stale, outside retention, or failed validation. The War Room refuses to invent a value.

Does a high radar score mean a token is safe?

No. Radar describes supported movement and attention evidence. It is not a calibrated risk or safety probability.

What does “pending proposal” mean?

Local metadata found a possible relationship between exact mints. It remains unverified until an operator explicitly reviews it.

Where is my watchlist stored?

In this browser’s local storage. It is not synced across devices and does not configure Telegram delivery.

Why can a coin disappear or change position?

The observed inventory, freshness, and supporting evidence change as new snapshots arrive. Filters and ranking lenses also affect what you see.

How do I verify the exact token?

Open the coin and compare the full mint address. Names, tickers, images, and narratives are not unique identifiers.
