Minimal data by design.

What the application processes

Pump War Room does not require an account, wallet, private key, payment, advertising identifier, or analytics cookie. Questions submitted to the local analyst are processed to answer the request and are not written to the application database.

Hosting and network infrastructure may temporarily process ordinary request metadata—such as IP address, user agent, timestamp, requested path, status code, and a request identifier—for delivery, reliability, abuse prevention, and security. Infrastructure providers may retain operational logs under their own policies.

Market data

The application stores public token identifiers plus derived outcome metrics and minimal provenance. It does not persist or expose bulk GeckoTerminal responses or candle series. Server-side provider requests contain public token and pool identifiers, not user account or wallet data.

Sharing and sale

The application does not sell personal information. Data may be processed by the configured hosting and upstream data providers only as needed to operate the service, comply with law, or protect security.

Retention and security

Application records are retained for operational research and recovery, subject to provider rights and deletion requirements. Database files are owner-only, and the linked Railway production volume is covered by Railway's stated AES-256 encryption at rest. Access is limited to the service operator and configured infrastructure. A provider purge requires exclusive database access, uses SQLite secure deletion and vacuuming, and verifies WAL cleanup; separate backups must be securely deleted independently. No internet service can promise absolute security.

You may stop using the service at any time. Questions about access, deletion, or this notice should be directed to the service operator through the same channel that provided access. Rights vary by location and will be honored where applicable.
