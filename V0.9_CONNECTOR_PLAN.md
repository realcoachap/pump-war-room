# v0.9 connector and authorization plan

Updated: 2026-08-09

## Release boundary

v0.9 proceeds with anonymous, on-chain early-actor intelligence from already approved PumpPortal/Solana evidence. Public wallet or platform identities, actor lookup, undocumented endpoint inspection, authenticated-cookie replay, page scraping, and connector-derived ranking, alerts, or trade influence remain prohibited.

Any social-feed connector must remain disabled until the source grants explicit written authorization for a documented API, webhook, or export. Before enablement, record the allowed fields, access method, rate limits, retention, attribution, display conditions, and revocation/deletion procedure, then add contract and failure-mode tests.
